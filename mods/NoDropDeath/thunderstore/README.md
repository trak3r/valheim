## What it does

When you die, you keep your inventory. An empty tombstone still appears, and the death map pin still drops — you just don't lose your stuff.

## Why install it

Vanilla death dumps everything into a gravestone and sends you on a corpse run. This keeps the grave marker for orientation, without the loot-pile punishment.

## How to use it

1. Install with BepInEx (same as other Valheim mods).
2. Die as usual.
3. Respawn with your items still on you. The empty tombstone and map pin are still there if you want them.

Does **not** stop death, skill loss, or food buffs falling off. Client-side — each player who wants this needs the mod.

No config.
