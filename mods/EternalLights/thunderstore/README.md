## What it does

Campfires, torches, braziers, and other fireplace-style lights stay lit forever — they never burn through resin, wood, or coal.

## Why install it

Vanilla lights go dark unless you keep feeding them fuel. This removes that busywork so your base stays lit without babysitting.

## How to use it

1. Install with BepInEx (same as other Valheim mods).
2. Place or use fireplace lights as usual.
3. Leave them alone — they stay fueled and burning.

Works for typical campfires, standing/wall torches, braziers, and similar. Does **not** fuel smelters, kilns, or cooking stations.

No config. Client-side only — works on any server.
