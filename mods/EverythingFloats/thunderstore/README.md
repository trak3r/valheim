## What it does

Dropped items get Valheim's Floating behavior so they bob on water instead of sinking.

## Why install it

Ore, wood, and gear lost overboard stay visible at the surface instead of vanishing into the deep.

## How to use it

1. Install with BepInEx.
2. Drop or knock items into water — they float at the waterline.

Does not stop despawn timers or auto-loot piles.

Client-side only — works on any server.
