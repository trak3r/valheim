## What it does

Thins Mistlands volumetric mist and clears a bit more space near you — still misty, just less of a white wall.

## Why install it

Vanilla Mistlands mist can bury the landscape. This keeps the look without deleting the biome feel.

## How to use it

1. Install with BepInEx.
2. Enter Mistlands — mist is thinner and you get a slightly larger clear pocket.

Does not change Misty whiteout weather (see No Ocean Fog).

Does not change demister / wisp rules.

No config. Client-side only.
